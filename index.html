03Projects

Multi-stage inspection of high-voltage insulators

Master’s project · YOLO · Aerial inspection

I evaluated whether locating insulators first and assessing their condition in individual crops improves defect detection in aerial images. The two-stage approach improved detection metrics in the RECPAD 2026 evaluation with 640-pixel inputs.

  1. Locate insulators
  2. Extract crops from the original image
  3. Assess insulator condition

Transfer learning and input resolution

YOLOv11 · Insulator Defect Image Dataset

I compared five YOLOv11 model sizes at three input resolutions. YOLOv11-L at 1280 pixels achieved the highest mean test mAP@50 in the comparison (0.892).
